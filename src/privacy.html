Privacy policy

Zaideih (zaideih.com) is a free music app for Zola, Myanmar, Mizo and Falam music. This page sets out what Zaideih keeps about the people who use it, why, who can see it, and how to have it deleted.

Last updated: 24 October 2026.

Listening, without an account

Listening needs no account. Without one, liked songs, playlists, play history and downloads are stored on your device and are not sent anywhere. Playing a song requests it from the Zaideih audio server, like loading any web page.

Play counts: after a song has played for 10 seconds, the app tells the Zaideih audio server which song was played and when, so the site can show how often songs are listened to. Nothing else is sent with it: no account, cookie or device identifier. Your network address is used only to stop the same sender from inflating counts, and is not stored.

Usage statistics (Google Analytics) are off unless you allow them on the About page. When allowed, they count page visits within Zaideih and song plays, and Google sets its own cookies for that. Searches and your library are never sent. The choice is kept on your device and can be changed at any time.

Signing in

An account is for keeping music of your own, your liked songs and playlists on every device, adding lyrics, and for catalog editors. You can sign in with Google or with GitHub; signing in with both, with the same verified email address, is one account. Once signed in, you can add a passkey, which signs you in with your device's face, fingerprint or code.

Your name and profile photo are shown only to you, on Account; Zaideih keeps the photo's address at Google or GitHub, not a copy of the photo, and it changes when you change it there. Your contacts and anything else in your Google or GitHub account are not kept. Nothing is posted to Google or GitHub on your behalf, except catalog corrections an editor or contributor chooses to send, which become public changes on GitHub in their name.

Signed in, your liked songs and playlists (which songs, the playlists' names, and when each was changed) are also kept with your account, so they are the same on every device where you sign in. What is on a device when you first sign in there joins them. Play history and downloads stay on the device. Deleting your account deletes them from the server; signing out keeps them on the device.

Lyrics you add or edit are public, as song lyrics. Each version is kept with your account and the time it was saved, so mistakes can be undone. Who saved a version is shown only to the people the owner has given the Lyrics right, not to listeners. When you report lyrics, your report (what you wrote, your account and when) is shown to those people too, and kept until they deal with it.

After signing in, your device keeps a sign-in token. The server keeps only a one-way fingerprint (hash) of it. It ends 90 days after you last use it, or when you sign out.

Google user data

Zaideih uses Google only for "Sign in with Google". In detail:

  • Access. When you sign in with Google, Zaideih asks for the basic sign-in permissions openid, email and profile, and receives your Google account id, your name, the address of your profile photo, your email address and whether Google has verified it. It does not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts, calendar, photos or any other Google data; it keeps the address of your profile photo, not a copy of it.
  • Use. This data is used only to sign you in: to recognise you when you come back, to show your name and profile photo to you on your account page, to show your name to the moderators of uploads, and to recognise that a Google and a GitHub sign-in with the same verified email address belong to the same person. It is not used for advertising of any kind, not used to build profiles, and not used to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • Sharing. Google user data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone. It is not transferred to third parties, except that it is stored with Cloudflare, the hosting provider that runs Zaideih, solely to provide the service. It is not disclosed to other users, apart from your name being visible to the moderators of uploads.
  • Protection. Data is sent only over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored in Cloudflare's database, which encrypts data at rest. Google's own sign-in tokens are used once, on the server, to read the details above, and are never stored or given to the app. Sign-in tokens issued by Zaideih are kept on the server only as one-way hashes. Stored email addresses and Google account ids are not shown in the app; only Zaideih's owner can read them, in the database.
  • Retention and deletion. Your Google account id, name, profile photo address and email are kept while your account exists, and deleted with it. You can remove the Google sign-in from your account at any time on Account, which deletes your Google account id, profile photo address and email at once (a name taken from Google stays as your account's name until the account is deleted); you can also revoke Zaideih's access at myaccount.google.com/connections. To have your whole account deleted, see "Your choices" below.

Zaideih's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your own music

Signed in, you can upload music to a private library that only you can play, or add files already kept in Zaideih's storage if the owner has given you that right. Zaideih keeps the files and the details you give (title, artist, album, year). Upload only recordings you are entitled to keep, such as music you bought or recorded.

Moderators chosen by the owner can see the titles of uploads and block accounts that upload spam or abusive files, or files reported by rights holders. Nobody else can see or play your library. If you ask for an album of yours to be published, a reviewer listens to it; once published it is part of the public catalog, credited to you.

Where it is kept, and for how long

Everything is kept by Cloudflare, which hosts Zaideih (its storage and database services), and is sent over encrypted connections. Account details are kept while the account exists. Uploaded files are kept until you delete them; a deleted upload is removed at once. Sign-in tokens end 90 days after their last use.

Your choices

  • Sign out on any device from Account; it removes the sign-in token there and on the server.
  • Remove a Google or GitHub sign-in from your account on the same page.
  • Delete any of your uploads at any time from Uploads in Library.
  • To have your account and everything kept for it deleted, open an issue asking for it (without personal details in it); it is done within 30 days. You can also revoke Zaideih's access at myaccount.google.com/connections (Google) or in your GitHub settings under Applications.

Changes and contact

This policy may change with new releases of the app; the date above shows the last change, and a change to how Google user data is used is announced in the app before it takes effect. Questions about it, and requests about your data: github.com/khensolomon/zaideih/issues.
