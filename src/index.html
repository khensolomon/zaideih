Zaideih music station

Zaideih is a free music app for Zola, Myanmar, Mizo and Falam music: thousands of albums, many of them old gospel and cassette recordings, to play in the browser and keep playing offline.

  • Listen without an account: search, browse by language and genre, make playlists and download songs to the device.
  • Sign in with Google or GitHub to keep music of your own in a private library, and to ask for an album of yours to be published for everyone. Signing in with Google gives Zaideih only your name, email address and Google account id, used only to recognise you; nothing else from your Google account is read, and nothing is shared or used for advertising.
  • Catalog editors correct titles and credits through GitHub; every change is reviewed.
